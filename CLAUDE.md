# Project rules (Tanuki Sensei)

## Assets and AI – general rule for all of the owner's projects
- Never use third-party assets (art, sprites, sounds, fonts, music) whose creator says they do not support AI use or that they are "for human-made projects only". This overrides the licence text (a CC0 licence does not make it acceptable).
  Known: **Kenney.nl assets must not be used.** RPG Maker default art is licensed for RPG Maker only. LPC parts have per-part licences and artist terms – check each.
- Art is either drawn by us in code (`sprites.js`, `people.js`, `art.js`) or generated by the owner with their own tools (their responsibility, tool terms permitting). Record every outside source in `docs/ASSETS.md`.

## Content correctness (most important requirement)
- All learner-facing Japanese and German lives in `content/` and passes `node tools/validate.mjs`. Nothing with status `draft` is used by the adaptive engine.
- New content goes through the external review: `node tools/make-review.mjs`, owner pastes the sheets into ChatGPT, Claude applies the answers (see `docs/REVIEW-LOG.md`).

## Build and release
- Signing key stays OUT of git (see `docs/ANDROID.md`). The owner holds `wolfsdorf-signing-key.jks`; ask for it again if a session starts fresh. Never change the applicationId (`de.wolfsdorf.lernspiel`) – updates depend on it. Bump `versionCode` for every release.
- Language of the app: German UI with Japanese content. The owner prefers working with Claude in English.

## UX preferences (from owner feedback)
- Thumb-friendly: actions at the bottom, bottom dock with script switch (漢|あ|A), left-hand mode, large fonts.
- Dark, bedtime-friendly colours; no bright whites. People must look friendly (no black-square eyes).

## Glossary (names the owner and Claude use)
- **Schriftwahl** (English: script switch) = the 漢 | あ | A buttons in the bottom dock. Must stay reachable on every screen, also while a menu sheet is open.
- **Dock** = the permanent bottom bar (← back, Schriftwahl, ふ furigana, DE hints, ☰ menu). Back stack + Android back button/swipe: see `navigateBack()` in `app.js`.
- **Tagesrunde** = the daily round (about 3 minutes, adaptive).
