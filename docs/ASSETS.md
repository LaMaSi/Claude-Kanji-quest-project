# Art assets and licences

**General rule (all projects):** never use art, sounds, fonts or other assets whose creator says they do not support AI use / that they are for human-made projects only – even if the licence text would technically allow it. When in doubt about a creator's stance, do not use the asset and ask the owner.

**Rule:** only art that is drawn by us (in code) or published under a licence that allows use in this project, including AI-assisted development.

## What is in the app today
- All sprites (`sprites.js`) and all people (`people.js`) are drawn in code for this project. No third-party art, so no licence questions.
- Scene pictures (`scenes/`) will come from the image program the owner uses; keep the generator's terms of use with the pictures.

## Why there is no ready-made character library
- Teaching vocabulary needs *parametric* faces (nose big/small, eyes narrow/wide, hair colours, clothes colours). Ready-made packs have fixed characters.
- **RPG Maker default art (RTP)**: licensed for use inside RPG Maker only – not usable here.
- **Universal LPC Spritesheet Generator**: every part has its own licence (CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, GPL, OGA-BY); attribution/share-alike apply and each artist's terms must be checked, including any AI-related terms.
- **Kenney.nl**: **do not use.** Kenney has said clearly that the assets are free for human-made projects only and that they do not support AI use. This is binding for us regardless of the licence text (CC0). (Corrected: an earlier note here called Kenney the safe option – that was wrong.)
- If outside art is added: record the source URL, author, licence and date in this file, and check the artist's page for restrictions on AI use.

## Sprite sheets for the character editor (in use since 0.5)
Generated by the owner with ChatGPT image generation from our templates and prompts (`docs/SPRITESHEET_BRIEF.md`), received 2026-11-23.
Sources are kept in `art/incoming/sheets/`. Used: `faces`, `hair_front`, `hair_back`, `eyes_A` (row 1: brown, blue, green), `features` (noses 1/4/6/7, mouths 10/12, eyebrows 28/35).
Not used (not matching the brief): `eyes_B`, `clothes_A`, `clothes_B` (fantasy costumes with baked-in colours), the other eye/feature variants.
Pipeline: `node tools/slice-sheets.mjs` (cut + remove magenta) -> `node tools/build-parts.mjs` (pick + pre-scale into `assets/people/`) -> `aipeople.js` (recolour + assemble). If the parts are missing the code-drawn portraits in `people.js` are used.

## Fonts
- **Klee One** (Fontworks, "Klee Project Authors"), SIL Open Font License 1.1 – handwriting-style font for the diary (Notizbuch), covers kanji, kana and Latin (romaji). Subset to the characters used: `tools/build-fonts.py` -> `assets/fonts/KleeOne-subset.woff2`; license text next to it (`assets/fonts/OFL-KleeOne.txt`). Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/kleeone . No statement against AI use found (checked 2026-11-23).
- Candidates **not yet used** because their licence/creator stance is not verified: Zen Kurenaido, Yomogi (Google Fonts, OFL). Check the creators' pages before adding.

## Yokai research (text)
`art/incoming/yokai_research.txt` is the text of the ChatGPT research document the owner supplied on 2026-11-23 ("Yokai-Freundschaften – Konzept- und Quest-Handbuch"). It was imported by `tools/import-yokai.py` into `content/yokai.json` (+ words, patterns, shops in `content/`). All imported texts have status `draft` until the external review sheet `review-3-yokai.md` has been answered. The yokai pictures in the Dex are placeholders drawn in code (`y_*` in `sprites.js`); real illustrations are welcome (see `docs/SCENES.md`).
