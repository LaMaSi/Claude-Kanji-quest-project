# Prompt for ChatGPT: yokai lore and quest design research

Paste this into a new ChatGPT conversation (use the research/browsing mode if you have it). Send the answer back to Claude.

```
I am building a calm, cozy Japanese-learning RPG for German-speaking beginners (about JLPT N5). The hero meets yokai and talks to them instead of fighting.
Each yokai has a habitat (one of: lake, mountains, shore/beach, woods, village, town, city) and a request: it wants a specific item or help. The player must understand
the request in simple Japanese, ask villagers where to find the item, travel there, collect it, and offer it politely. Afterwards the yokai becomes a friend and gets a lore card.

Please research and answer in German (Japanese terms in kanji + kana + rōmaji):
1. Suggest 12 yokai that fit this idea, mixing well-known ones (kappa, kitsune, tanuki, tengu, yuki-onna, …) with a few gentle lesser-known ones. For each: name (kanji, kana,
   rōmaji, German), best habitat from my list, what the folklore says it likes, wants or fears, and a short lore card (3-4 sentences) suitable for adults.
2. For each yokai a quest: the request, the item and where it can be found or bought (it should be an everyday item from my list: cucumber, fried tofu, sake, peach, fish,
   rice cake, tea, medicine, apple, bread, egg, meat), 2 short hint sentences an NPC could say in very simple Japanese (N5 vocabulary and grammar only: は, が, を, に, です, ます, ください, あります/います),
   the polite phrase the player says when offering the item, and one cultural detail (a custom, a taboo, a bow, a greeting).
3. Mark clearly what is well-attested folklore, what is a regional variant, and what is modern pop-culture invention. Name your sources (books, museums, encyclopedias).
4. Avoid anything offensive, sacred-ritual misuse or disrespect toward real religious practice (Shinto/Buddhist); suggest respectful wording where in doubt.
5. List the N5-level Japanese words my game would additionally need for all quests (place, item and action words) as a table: kanji, kana, German.
Give the result as tables, no long introduction.
```
